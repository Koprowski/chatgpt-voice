# Legal and terms

## Account requirements

ChatGPT dictation requires an account with access to voice dictation in the ChatGPT web interface. Gemini dictation requires a Google account with access to Gemini voice input at `https://gemini.google.com/`.

This project is not affiliated with OpenAI or Google. We do not guarantee availability of either provider's web dictation feature for any account type.

## Provider terms of use

This app automates supported web interfaces: it uses a browser (Playwright) to open the selected provider, trigger the built-in voice input, and programmatically read the transcribed text from the page so it can be pasted into other applications.

OpenAI's terms may restrict automated extraction from their services. Google/Gemini terms may also restrict automated access or extraction. Using this app could therefore be considered a violation of a provider's terms. Enforcement is up to the provider; they may take action, such as warning or restricting an account, if they treat this use as non-compliant.

You use this software at your own risk. By using it, you are responsible for your own compliance with provider terms and applicable law. The authors of this project do not encourage or endorse violating any terms of service.

If you prefer to stay clearly within a provider's terms, use that provider's official interface without automation, or use an official API/product if one is available for your use case.
